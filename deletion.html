Data Deletion

Your data, your choice

You can request deletion of your personal data at any time.

How to Request Deletion

  • Send an email to support@orbriot.com with subject "Data Deletion Request"
  • Include your username or email used for account
  • We will process your request within 30 days

What Will Be Deleted

  • Game progress and scores
  • Account information
  • Purchase history
  • All stored preferences

Note: Some data may be retained for legal obligations or fraud prevention purposes.

Questions? Contact us at support@orbriot.com
