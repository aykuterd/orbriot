How to Play

Master the basics and become a brick-breaking legend

1

Aim & Launch

Drag and release to launch your ball. Use the aiming line to predict the trajectory.

2

Break Bricks

Hit bricks to break them. Each hit reduces brick HP. Collect falling gems for bonus rewards.

3

Use Power-Ups

Activate power-ups strategically to clear more bricks and maximize your score.

4

Don't Drop!

Keep the ball from falling off the bottom. Use your skills to survive as long as possible.
